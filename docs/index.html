How the score works

Betty follows the method Yuka describes publicly. It is an independent estimate, not an official Yuka score.

PartWeightFrom
Nutrition60%Nutri-Score (original 2017 algorithm), converted with Yuka's published table
Additives30%Risk levels from Open Food Facts/EFSA plus a small curated list; one high-risk additive caps the product at 49
Organic10%Bonus for an official organic label
  • Only certified-organic products can go above 90; a perfect non-organic food is exactly 90.
  • Nutri-Score D or E means the score can't go above 49.
  • Bands: Excellent 90+, Very good 75+, Good 50+, Bad 25+, Very bad below 25.

Halal: "Halal certified" means a certifier label is on the pack. "Halal (ingredients)" only means nothing haram or doubtful was found in the ingredient list; it is not a certification. Gelatin, rennet, carmine, L-cysteine, some emulsifiers, glycerin, animal fat and wine vinegar are flagged doubtful unless the pack is certified or names a halal source (e.g. fish gelatin, microbial enzyme). Flavourings and vanilla extract get a "check" note. Kosher needs a certification label. Allergy filters are cautious: no ingredient list or a "may contain" warning means the product is left out. With a real allergy, always read the pack.
